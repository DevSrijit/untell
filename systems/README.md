# Design systems

Choose one system for a video. Then read its `SYSTEM.md` and use only what it names.
Ten systems are light and ten are dark.

## Choose by purpose and tone, not by subject

A technical subject does not need a technical system. An explainer about HTTPS can be calm and exact
(Studio), printed and warm (Riso), friendly (Pop) or engineered (Schematic). Ask three questions:

1. **What is the video for?** Use the "Use it for" column: a launch, a kinetic lyric, a quote card, a tutorial.
2. **How should the viewer feel?** Calm and premium (Studio, Keynote, Ma, Nocturne), exact and technical
   (Spec, Schematic, Graphite, Console), loud and fast (Kinetic, Courtside, Raw), warm and made by hand
   (Riso, Cutout, Primary), friendly (Pop), cinematic (Noir, Title, Matte).
3. **Light or dark?** Half the systems are each. A feed full of dark explainers makes a light one stand out.

When two systems fit, take the one the brief would not expect. Write your reason in one sentence.

| System | Mode | Use it for | Character |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| [Studio](studio/SYSTEM.md) | light | launch, app-demo, ad, presentation | Quiet and exact. One subject, a lot of space, one sentence at a time. |
| [Spec](spec/SYSTEM.md) | light | changelog, launch, data-story, tutorial | A technical drawing of a product. Every element sits on the grid, labelled. |
| [Manual](manual/SYSTEM.md) | light | tutorial, explainer, app-demo | A patient teacher. Code on one side, the explanation on the other, one line lit at a time. |
| [Ma](ma/SYSTEM.md) | light | quote-card, launch, title-sequence, presentation | Silence. One small word or image in a large empty field, held long. |
| [Primary](primary/SYSTEM.md) | light | explainer, ad, title-sequence, presentation | Geometry that explains. Circles, squares and triangles move in 90-degree steps. |
| [Riso](riso/SYSTEM.md) | light | music-lyric, ad, title-sequence, loop | Printed by hand. Off-register layers stamp in one after another. |
| [Cutout](cutout/SYSTEM.md) | light | title-sequence, trailer, logo-sting, quote-card | One idea per shape. Flat cut paper slides, rotates and builds the title. |
| [Mark](mark/SYSTEM.md) | light | logo-sting, launch, title-sequence | Construction. The mark assembles from simple modules, then holds. |
| [Raw](raw/SYSTEM.md) | light | ad, trailer, changelog, presentation | Unpolished on purpose, exact in its spacing. Text appears in one frame. |
| [Pop](pop/SYSTEM.md) | light | ad, app-demo, explainer, wrapped | Friendly and bouncy. Big rounded shapes land with squash and stretch. |
| [Keynote](keynote/SYSTEM.md) | dark | launch, ad, trailer | Theatre. A single subject emerges from black and is lit, slowly. |
| [Graphite](graphite/SYSTEM.md) | dark | app-demo, changelog, launch | A calm, dense product shown honestly. The UI is the hero; the camera tilts slightly. |
| [Console](console/SYSTEM.md) | dark | tutorial, app-demo, changelog, ad | Something is running. Commands type at a human rate and the output answers. |
| [Noir](noir/SYSTEM.md) | dark | trailer, quote-card, title-sequence | Mystery. Hard light cuts across the type; shadows slide. |
| [Title](title/SYSTEM.md) | dark | trailer, title-sequence, quote-card | Restraint. A small line appears without animation and holds in silence. |
| [Matte](matte/SYSTEM.md) | dark | launch, ad | Material. The object turns under a passing light; type is small and quiet. |
| [Kinetic](kinetic/SYSTEM.md) | dark | kinetic-type, music-lyric, ad, title-sequence | Type is the motion. Words fill the frame and cut on the beat. |
| [Courtside](courtside/SYSTEM.md) | dark | ad, wrapped, trailer | Momentum. A condensed headline slams over a subject; cuts whip on the beat. |
| [Schematic](schematic/SYSTEM.md) | dark | explainer, data-story, tutorial, presentation | Engineering. Systems drawn in white hairlines, labelled and measured. |
| [Nocturne](nocturne/SYSTEM.md) | dark | quote-card, presentation, captioned-clip, launch | A considered statement. Words fade in one by one in a large serif. |
